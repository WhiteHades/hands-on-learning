# Welcome

Hands-on Learning keeps the lesson, your code, and local feedback in one
keyboard-first terminal workspace.

This demo has three stops:

1. Read this short orientation.
2. Edit and check a C program.
3. Answer one review question.

Use `j` and `k` to move, `Space l l` to choose a lesson, and `?` to open the
keybinding guide. Your edits and progress stay on this machine.
